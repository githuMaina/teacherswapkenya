How Teacher Swap Kenya works
A free platform helping TSC teachers find mutual transfer partners.
1
Register your request — Enter your name, phone, level, subjects and current posting. Select the subcounties you want to transfer to across any county borders.
2
Browse open requests — See all teachers seeking transfers. Phone and school are hidden until a match is confirmed.
3
Find your match — The engine finds 2-way swaps and triangle swaps (3 teachers rotating in a closed cycle).
4
Contact your match — A confirmed match reveals phone numbers and school names. Call or WhatsApp to agree on the swap.
5
Lodge at TSC together — All parties file the mutual transfer request at the official TSC portal. Teacher Swap Kenya found the match — TSC completes the process.
What is a triangle swap?

Sometimes no direct 2-way match exists. A triangle swap involves 3 teachers: Teacher A moves to Teacher B's subcounty, B moves to C's subcounty, and C moves to A's subcounty. Everyone moves, nobody is left out. All three file a joint transfer at TSC.
